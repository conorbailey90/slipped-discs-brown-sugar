Brilliant coffee, proper cafe snacks, and a rack of records to dig through.

We’re a much-loved independent cafe on Billericay High Street with a record shop built right in. Order a silky flat white or a proper hot chocolate, settle into a table inside or grab a sunny seat out front, then lose twenty happy minutes flicking through vinyl you weren’t planning to buy. Everyone does it.

The shop

Two halves of the same room

One side is the cafe: warm tables inside, a serious coffee machine and a counter groaning with cake, plus a handful of sunny seats out front for watching the High Street go by. There’s a comfy spot whatever the weather, indoors or out. The other side is the record shop: crates of new pressings up front, secondhand gems and bargain bins along the wall, CDs stacked underneath, and a shelf of gifts, tote bags and turntable odds and ends.

No pressure to buy, no hovering. Bring a laptop, bring the dog, bring your nan. Ask us to spin something on the deck and we probably will.

Loyalty card
